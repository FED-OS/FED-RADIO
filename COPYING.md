## What this means for your build

- **Your fork is yours.** MIT lets you keep changes private or share them —
  no copyleft obligations on your station catalog or audio files.
- **Your content stays yours.** The license covers the *code and docs*.
  Your station titles, audio, and any YouTube links you author are your
  content, not part of the licensed work.
- **Attribution appreciated, not enforced.** A link back to this repo when
  you fork is classy, not mandatory.
