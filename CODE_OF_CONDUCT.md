# Code of Conduct — FED-Radio

## Our pledge

FED-Radio is a garage-built project, and the garage stays open to everyone.
We pledge to keep every interaction in this project — issues, pull requests,
discussions, chat, and in-person events — free of harassment and hostile
behavior, for everyone regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that keeps the broadcast healthy:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
* Focusing on what's best for the community
* Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

* Harassment, trolling, or insulting/derogatory comments
* Public or private harassment of any kind
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
* Any conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional or
  community setting

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior
and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code. Repeat
offenders may be banned.

## Scope

This code applies wherever the project is represented: the repository, its
issues and pull requests, GitHub Discussions, and any project-affiliated space.

## Enforcement

Report abuse to the maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
All complaints are reviewed and investigated confidentially. Maintainers who
do not follow or enforce the code in good faith may face temporary or
permanent removal from the project.
